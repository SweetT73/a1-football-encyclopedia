- **February 10, 1954 — contemporary Associated Press report in the *Big Spring Daily Herald*:** Rice's spring-training report specifically names **Layton Golemon and Lavon Cox as the two returning tackle lettermen** among the Owls' 13 returning lettermen. This is **individually named, contemporary evidence of his 1953 Rice letterman status and continued 1954 squad role**. The report says six of seven 1953 starting linemen had departed and identifies Kenny Paul as the lone returning starter; **do not infer that Golemon started the 1953 Cotton Bowl or every game**. [Original February 10, 1954 newspaper issue](https://newspapers.swco.ttu.edu/bitstream/handle/20.500.12255/12803/The_Big_Spring_Daily_Herald__1954-02-10.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3).
