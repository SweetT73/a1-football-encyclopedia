# Kyle Bryant — A1 permanent individual research record

## Documented historical football evidence

Senior Texas A&M kicker from A&M Consolidated High School, named **special-teams MVP** at the banquet following the 1997 football season. The university lists then-school career records of **60 field goals**, **145 made extra points on 152 attempts**, **325 points**, and **.706 field-goal accuracy**. These are historical record claims as of February 2, 1998, not current school records.

**Original source:** Texas A&M University Athletics, “Football Award Banquet Honorees,” published **February 2, 1998**: https://12thman.com/news/1998/02/02/football-award-banquet-honorees . The banquet followed the 1997 football season; do not misdate the award as a separate 1998 season achievement.
