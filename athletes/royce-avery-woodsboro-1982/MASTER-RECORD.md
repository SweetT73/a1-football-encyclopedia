# Royce Avery — Woodsboro (1982–1983)

Official UIL historical results identify Royce Avery of Woodsboro as the 1982 Class 2A boys 400-meter dash state champion (48.74 seconds) and 1983 champion (47.64 seconds). Source: https://wwwprod.uiltexas.org/track-field/historical-archive .
