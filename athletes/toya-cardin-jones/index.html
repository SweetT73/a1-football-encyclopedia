Career Highlights

1998 · TEXAS A&M · #5

The Championship Season

Wearing #5 for Coach R. C. Slocum’s defense, Jones was a key part of the secondary. Alongside teammate Dat Nguyen, he recovered a pivotal fourth-quarter fumble against Missouri that set up the game-winning field goal.

1998 · BIG 12 CHAMPIONSHIP

The Goal-Line Stand

Jones made the game-saving tackle on a Hail Mary pass at the one-yard line as regulation expired against Kansas State. Texas A&M went on to win the championship in double overtime.

1997 · NCAA CHAMPIONS

Track Royalty

A decorated sprinter, long jumper and All-American, Jones ran on Texas A&M’s 1997 NCAA champion 4×100-meter relay team.

The Life and Career of Toya Cardin Jones

From Refugio to Texas A&M, professional football, and the transportation business he founded, Jones built a career across several fields. His individual achievements are told below in career order, with dated results and discreet numbered footnotes.

High School Football — Seasons and Honors

At Refugio High School, Jones developed into one of Texas’s most decorated small-school defenders. Contemporary selections trace his rise from a second-team All-State defensive back in 1993 to first-team recognition and a defensive player-of-the-year honor in 1994.

TEXAS A&M UNIVERSITY · COLLEGE FOOTBALLTexas A&M — Football

At Texas A&M, Jones contributed on defense and special teams across four seasons. His senior year culminated in the 1998 Big 12 Championship, where his tackle of Everett Burnett at the one-yard line kept Kansas State from scoring on the final play of regulation.

1995 — Texas A&M football
Four-year college football letter sequence begins.
September 2, 1995 vs LSU — confirmed game participation, jersey No. 5, one solo tackle (1-0), no assisted tackles; the official [36] independently confirms the earlier A1 entry.
This is a verified college game appearance and must not be projected backward to his 1991–1994 high-school games.
1996 — Texas A&M football25 tackles (15 solo, 10 assisted); one fumble recovery returned seven yards, as preserved in the earlier A1 biography.
1997 — Texas A&M football
August 27, 1997, post-scrimmage depth chart: Texas A&M listed No. 5 Toya Jones OR No. 30 Brandon Jennings at first-string free safety; this establishes a shared/alternative depth-chart designation, not a verified regular-season start.[37].
12 games; 30 tackles; two forced fumbles in the [38].
Earlier research also records one interception; Texas A&M’s [39] independently confirms his selection as 1997 special-teams captain.
1998 — Texas A&M football13 games, 18 tackles (15 solo, 3 assisted); Special Teams MVP/captain, confirmed by [40]; its original report also confirms 18 tackles and the Missouri fumble recovery. Sept. 12: helped down a Shane Lechler 69-yard punt at Louisiana Tech 1 ([41]). Nov. 14: recovered muffed Missouri punt to set up winning field goal. Dec. 5: tackled Everett Burnett at 1-yard line at end of regulation of Big 12 Championship; A&M won 36–33 in double OT. Kansas State entered the championship ranked No. 2 with an unbeaten regular-season record and featured Heisman runner-up quarterback Michael Bishop, receiver Aaron Lockett (Tyler Lockett’s uncle), future NFL kicker Martin Gramatica, and future NFL defenders Darren Howard, Mark Simoneau and Ben Leber. Gramatica made four field goals in this game. These opponents provide context for the significance of Jones’s stop; they are not credited as Jones’s individual statistics. [42]; [43].

October 26, 2026 — Source and Photograph Verification Addendum

Contemporary reporting confirms Toya Cardin Jones died October 22, 2026, at age 49, six days before his fiftieth birthday. KRIS 6 identifies him as a Refugio High School Class of 1995 graduate and credits him with 17 UIL state-meet medals, including 13 gold, the 1997 NCAA 4 × 100-meter relay championship at Texas A&M, the 1998 Big 12 football championship, and induction into the Texas Track and Field Hall of Fame in 2020. These findings supplement rather than replace the historical chronology above.

Additional contemporary reporting identifies the November 14, 1998 Missouri punt-return fumble recovery and its contribution to Texas A&M's 17–14 win. The original AP caption names Toya Jones (#5) alongside Dat Nguyen. This caption is a candidate for photograph verification; no new image is republished here.

Photograph identification control: Two previously suggested images are expressly excluded from the confirmed Toya collection: the displayed Houston Texans training-camp photograph with unverified player identities, and the photograph described as Toya waving to a crowd. The existence of a Houston Chronicle caption naming Toya in a separate #27 training-camp photograph does not authenticate the image previously displayed. Do not reuse either rejected image without independent matching provenance.

League statistics: Pro Football Archives records Birmingham Thunderbolts safety #13 in 10 XFL games (five tackles, one interception, one punt return for minus one yard). Preserve any more detailed game logs already documented elsewhere on this page. NFL training-camp and preseason participation must not be represented as NFL regular-season appearances.
