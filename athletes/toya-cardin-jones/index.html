2010 UIL All-Century Recognition — Two Individual Honors

UIL Centennial fan vote: No. 2 all-century selection in both the 100m and 200m

On May 12, 2010, the University Interscholastic League announced its fan-selected All-Century Boys Track & Field Teams after more than 4,700 votes. Toya Jones of Refugio was selected second in the 100-meter dash (UIL listing: 10.44 seconds) and second in the 200-meter dash (UIL listing: 21.04 seconds). These are retrospective all-century recognition rankings, not finishes in a 2010 competition. Original UIL announcement, May 12, 2010.

1994 source reconciliation: The UIL's original 1994 state-meet result lists Jones winning the 100m in 10.2 (record) and 200m in 20.8 (tying the Class 2A record), while the UIL's later all-century/season-record listings give 10.44 and 21.04 respectively. Preserve both published source-specific versions; the timing/record-method explanation is not established. Original 1994 100m result · Original 1994 200m result.

Remembrance

Toya Cardin Jones died October 22, 2026, at age 49, six days before his 50th birthday. His legacy spans track records and championships, international competition, football in multiple leagues, entrepreneurship and service.
