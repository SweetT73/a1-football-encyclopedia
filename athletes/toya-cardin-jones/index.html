Texas A&M — Football (1995–1998)

4 letters · 87 career tackles · 1998 Big 12 champion · Special Teams MVP / Captain

1995: One solo tackle vs LSU (September 2).
1996: 25 tackles (15 solo, 10 assisted), fumble recovery returned 7 yards.
1997: 12 games, one interception; Special Teams Captain recognition.
1998: 13 games, 18 tackles (15 solo, 3 assisted); Special Teams MVP and Captain; recovered muffed punt at Missouri on November 14 to set up winning field goal; December 5 Big 12 Championship final-play tackle of Everett Burnett at one-yard line, Texas A&M winning 36–33 in double overtime.

Texas A&M — Track and Field

4 letters · NCAA national champion · Collegiate All-American · 38.80 championship relay

1997 undefeated NCAA champion 4×100m relay: College Station Relays 39.95; Baylor/Dr Pepper Invitational 39.57; Texas A&M–Texas dual 40.02; Texas Relays 39.04; Penn Relays 38.92; Big 12 Championships 39.30; NCAA Outdoor Championships Bloomington 38.80, national champions and Haught Track Complex record.
Relay: Micheal/Michael Price, Toya Jones (second leg), Billy/Bill Fobbs, Danny McCray; spellings differ among historical accounts.

2002: Houston Texans, four preseason games and one documented tackle; no regular-season NFL appearance established.

Remembrance

Toya Cardin Jones died October 22, 2026, at age 49, six days before his 50th birthday. His legacy spans track records and championships, international competition, football in multiple leagues, entrepreneurship and service.
