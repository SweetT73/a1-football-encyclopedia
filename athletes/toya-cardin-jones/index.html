Refugio Remembers Toya Cardin Jones

Reported by Raegan Garvin · KRIS 6 News · Corpus Christi, Texas

A community remembers a remarkable two-sport athlete whose achievements carried him from Refugio High School to Texas A&M, professional football and the Texas Track & Field Hall of Fame. Garvin’s television tribute brings Toya’s story to life through local reporting and historical sports footage.

The original KRIS 6 report is the place to watch the station’s presentation and its archival track-and-field footage. A1 credits Garvin and KRIS 6 for the reporting; the original source and reuse rights for the historical clips have not yet been established.

1998 — Texas A&M football

13 games, 18 tackles (15 solo, 3 assisted); Special Teams MVP/captain, confirmed by [40]; its original report also confirms 18 tackles and the Missouri fumble recovery. Sept. 12: helped down a Shane Lechler 69-yard punt at Louisiana Tech 1 ([41]). Nov. 14: recovered muffed Missouri punt to set up winning field goal. Dec. 5: tackled Everett Burnett at 1-yard line at end of regulation of Big 12 Championship; A&M won 36–33 in double OT. Kansas State entered the championship ranked No. 2 with an unbeaten regular-season record and featured Heisman runner-up quarterback Michael Bishop, receiver Aaron Lockett (Tyler Lockett’s uncle), future NFL kicker Martin Gramatica, and future NFL defenders Darren Howard, Mark Simoneau and Ben Leber. Gramatica made four field goals in this game. These opponents provide context for the significance of Jones’s stop; they are not credited as Jones’s individual statistics. [42]; [43].

Texas A&M · Football & Track

1997 · NCAA CHAMPIONS

Track Royalty

A decorated sprinter, long jumper and All-American, Jones ran on Texas A&M’s 1997 NCAA champion 4×100-meter relay team.

1998 · TEXAS A&M · #5

The Championship Season

Wearing #5 for Coach R. C. Slocum’s defense, Jones was a key part of the secondary. Alongside teammate Dat Nguyen, he recovered a pivotal fourth-quarter fumble against Missouri that set up the game-winning field goal.

1998 · BIG 12 CHAMPIONSHIP

The Goal-Line Stand

Jones made the game-saving tackle on a Hail Mary pass at the one-yard line as regulation expired against Kansas State. Texas A&M went on to win the championship in double overtime.

RECOGNITION & BUSINESS

2010–2024

May 12, 2010 — UIL All-Century honors

Selected No. 2 in both the boys’ 100-meter and 200-meter dashes on the University Interscholastic League’s fan-voted All-Century Boys Track & Field Teams. UIL listed Jones of Refugio with 10.44 (100m) and 21.04 (200m).
The official release says more than 4,700 votes were cast across the project; rankings reflect a fan vote, not a new race or statistical performance.[56].

2020 — track Hall of Fame

Inducted into Texas Track & Field Hall of Fame January 10, 2020.[45].

2017 / 2023 / 2024 — business

Worked in sales at Sagis Diagnostics in Houston before founding Sagis Transport in Sugar Land on March 6, 2017 (precise founding date in [57]), per the [35].
Aggie 100 No. 10 in 2023 (119.90% growth) and No. 20 in 2024 (82.61% growth), independently listed by [57]. A [58] additionally reports that the company grew to more than 300 employees across 43 states; these operating-scale figures are attributed to the memorial and are not independently verified by a dated company filing.

2024 — Texas Sports Hall of Fame nomination

Named in the Texas Sports Hall of Fame’s published 2024 nomination submissions, with Refugio High School, Texas A&M, and defensive back identified in the entry.[60] This documents a nomination, not Hall of Fame induction.

BEYOND THE FIELD · WORK, FRIENDSHIP & FAMILY

THE MAN WHO LIFTED PEOPLE UP

“He could make you feel seen, hype you up and make you laugh”— From a coworker’s Facebook tribute supplied by Sweet T; author’s name awaiting confirmation.

A coworker who attended Texas A&M with Toya and worked alongside him for more than ten years remembered his jokes, generosity and listening ear. Her tribute describes the pride he took in his family and in bringing his mother dinner every Sunday. She credits him with growing Sagis Transport from a local logistics business into more than 40 U.S. markets; that description remains attributed to her remembrance.
