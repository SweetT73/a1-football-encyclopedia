# Ray Hutchinson — A1 permanent individual record

**School:** Refugio High School, Texas.

## Documented honor
- **1982 Texas Sports Writers Class 3A second-team All-State defensive back.** Listed as "Hutchinson, Ray — Refugio — D-Back" in the 1982 TXSW Class 3A historical roster.
- Source: Billy Wilbanks, compiler (October 5, 2010), https://txswa.org/All-State%201921-2010/3-Football-1981-1995.pdf .

This compiled selection does not establish individual interceptions, tackles, starts, or specific championship-game plays. No authentic photograph was available from the source. No conversations are certified cleared.

## 1983 Texas football signing

The February 9, 1983 *Hereford Brand* Associated Press signing list identifies Ray Hutchinson of Refugio, defensive back, 5 ft 11 in, 170 lb, under University of Texas signees. This documents the reported signing, not varsity playing time. Source: https://newspapers.swco.ttu.edu/bitstream/handle/20.500.12255/72776/ttu_herfrd_007954.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3 .

## 1983 high-school track record

Texas A&M–Kingsville's April 26, 2023 *Javelina Highlights* (p. 11, Region IV-4A meet historical records) lists **Ray Hutchinson, Refugio, 1983, boys 110-meter hurdles, 13.45 seconds**. This is a documented historical regional-meet record listing, not a UIL state-meet final and not evidence of his college hurdling career. The original 1983 meet date and wind are not given. Source: https://s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/sidearm.nextgen.sites/tamuk.sidearmsports.com/documents/2023/4/26/highlights875.pmd.pdf .
