# Ray Hutchinson — A1 permanent individual record

**School:** Refugio High School, Texas.

## Documented honor
- **1982 Texas Sports Writers Class 3A second-team All-State defensive back.** Listed as "Hutchinson, Ray — Refugio — D-Back" in the 1982 TXSW Class 3A historical roster.
- Source: Billy Wilbanks, compiler (October 5, 2010), https://txswa.org/All-State%201921-2010/3-Football-1981-1995.pdf .

This compiled selection does not establish individual interceptions, tackles, starts, or specific championship-game plays. No authentic photograph was available from the source. No conversations are certified cleared.

The February 9, 1983 *Hereford Brand* Associated Press signing list identifies Ray Hutchinson of Refugio, defensive back, 5 ft 11 in, 170 lb, under University of Texas signees.
