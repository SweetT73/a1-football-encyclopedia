# William Jones — A1 permanent individual record

**School:** Refugio High School, Texas. The 1987 roster lists him as a junior, and the 1988 roster as a senior.

## Individually documented honors
- **1987 Texas Sports Writers Class 2A first-team All-State defensive lineman**, Refugio; listed as junior, 250 lb.
- **1988 Texas Sports Writers Class 2A first-team All-State offensive center**, Refugio; listed as senior.
- **1988 Texas Sports Writers Class 2A second-team All-State defensive lineman**, Refugio; listed as senior.

**Source:** Billy Wilbanks, *Texas H.S. All-State Football Players by Year*, 1981–1995 compilation (October 5, 2010), 1987 and 1988 TXSW 2A rosters: https://txswa.org/All-State%201921-2010/3-Football-1981-1995.pdf .

These are position-specific selections, not evidence of individual game statistics. Do not confuse William Jones with Toya Cardin Jones or Ralph Jones. No authentic photo verified from this source; no conversations cleared.
