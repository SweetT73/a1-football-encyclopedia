# Eugene Whitmire — A1 permanent individual record

**School:** Refugio High School, Texas.

## Verified selection
- **1982 Texas Sports Writers Class 3A first-team All-State offensive back.** The 2010 Billy Wilbanks historical roster transcription lists "Whitmire, Eugene — Refugio — O-Back" under the 1982 TXSW 3A first team.
- Source: https://txswa.org/All-State%201921-2010/3-Football-1981-1995.pdf (1982 TXSW Class 3A roster).

The historical compilation does not establish game appearances, rushing or passing statistics, or exact backfield assignment. No verified photograph accompanies this source; no conversations are cleared for deletion.

## Original 1983 Texas Relays — individually named relay participation

- **April 6–9, 1983, Austin, high-school boys 400-meter relay (4×100 m), event 42, Heat 1:** University of Texas's original results explicitly name **Eugene Whitmire** as a Refugio relay runner with **Ray Hutchinson, Maurice Shaw and Kent Harris**. The team placed **fourth in its preliminary heat**, not fourth in the final. The fourth-place line appears to show **43.58 seconds** (43.4 hand timing), but its scanned column alignment is imperfect; treat the exact time as provisional until verified against the page image.
- **Primary source:** [Original University of Texas 1983 Texas Relays meet results, event 42, Heat 1](https://texaslonghorns.com/documents/download/2019/6/19/TEXASRELAYS83Cont2.pdf). This is independent track participation, **not evidence of an additional 1982 football play, start or honor**. No authentic photograph was added.
