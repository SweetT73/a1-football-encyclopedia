# Kent Harris — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Kent Harris, Refugio High School, Texas; identified in the University of Texas's original April 1983 Texas Relays boys competition results. Do not conflate him with unrelated athletes sharing this name.

## Individually documented 1983 Texas Relays results

- **High-school boys pole vault, final:** **Third place, 14 ft 6 in**. The original results list **David Gatch** first (16 ft 0 in), **Terry Bowden** second (15 ft 0 in), and **Kent Harris of Refugio** third (14 ft 6 in). This is a **Texas Relays placement**, not a UIL state championship result.
- **High-school boys 400-meter relay (4×100 m), event 42, Heat 1:** Harris is expressly listed on Refugio's team alongside **Ray Hutchinson, Eugene Whitmire, and Maurice Shaw**; **fourth in the preliminary heat**, not fourth overall. The original printed fourth-place time appears **43.58 seconds (43.4 hand timing)**, but the scan's text alignment makes that exact time provisional until page-image confirmation.
- **High-school boys 1600-meter relay (4×400 m), event 51, Heat 1:** Harris is expressly listed with **Sterling Scott, David Lewis, and Jeffrey Dickerson**; **fourth in the preliminary heat, 3:30.84**. Do not report this as a final-round placing or a solo 400-meter time.

**Primary source for all three results:** [University of Texas Athletics, original 1983 Texas Relays results, April 6–9, 1983](https://texaslonghorns.com/documents/download/2019/6/19/TEXASRELAYS83Cont2.pdf), events 31, 42 and 51. Source reviewed October 31, 2026.
