# Edward Lewis — Refugio 1968 — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Edward Lewis, football end on the **1968 Refugio High School** team in Texas. He is separately named from **Eugene “Bull” Lewis** on the same newspaper page. Neither is established as **Calvin Lewis**, the distinct A1 subject whose identity remains unresolved.

## Original 1968 season statistics

The *Refugio Timely Remarks* of **December 5, 1968, page 8**, prints a cumulative **12-game** receiving table listing **Edward Lewis with 9 catches, 49 yards and 1 receiving touchdown**. The individual scoring table lists **8 points** for Edward Lewis. These are season-to-date figures, not a single-game stat line.

The article also reports Refugio's **21–19** November 29, 1968 victory over Belton. It does **not** explicitly identify Edward Lewis as making a particular play in that game; do not infer his participation or an individual reception in the Belton contest.

**Original source:** *Refugio Timely Remarks*, vol. 51, no. 12, Thursday, December 5, 1968, p. 8, “Bobcat Rally Wins Belton Game 21-19” and accompanying 1968 cumulative statistical tables: https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth651690/m1/8/ .

## Safeguards

No relationship to Eugene “Bull” Lewis or Calvin Lewis is established. Do not transfer either man's achievements into this record. No authentic individual photograph was recovered and no conversation is certified cleared.
