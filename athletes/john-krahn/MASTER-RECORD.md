# John Krahn — A1 permanent individual record

## Identity and original contemporary football evidence
- **John Krahn**, also called **“Junior”**, high-school football offensive lineman at **Martin Luther King High School**, Riverside, California.
- In **September–October 2015**, contemporaneous CBS Los Angeles and Associated Press reporting described him as a **7-foot-tall, approximately 440-pound high-school senior**. The Associated Press report identifies him as the **starting right tackle** for King High's Wolves. These are **2015 reported measurements and role**, not permanent measurements or professional football statistics.
- **CBS Los Angeles, September 25, 2015:** https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/meet-the-worlds-biggest-football-player/
- **Associated Press, Andrew Dalton, October 6, 2015**, carried by *Portland Press Herald*: https://www.pressherald.com/2015/10/06/california-high-school-linemans-stats-7-feet-440-pounds/

## Subsequent prep-football follow-up
- A **2016 MaxPreps follow-up** describes Krahn at Georgia Preparatory Sports Academy, **approximately 7 ft and 410 lb**; he discussed a move from left tackle to right guard and having played across the line, including center. The 2015 and 2016 weight/position reports refer to different periods and must not be combined as one season's measurements.
- Original interview/follow-up: https://www.maxpreps.com/news/w5sPOcg4ZU-4aQQQLNoWIA/catching-up-with-the-worlds-largest-football-player%2C-7-foot%2C-410-pound-john-krahn.htm .
