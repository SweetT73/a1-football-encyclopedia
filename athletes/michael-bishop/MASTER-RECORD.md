# Michael Bishop — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity and scope:** Michael Bishop, high-school football player from **Willis, Texas** and later **Kansas State quarterback**, documented in two distinct original historical football sources that intersect the Toya Cardin Jones record.

## 1995 high-school All-Star selection

- *Polk County Enterprise*, **July 30, 1995, p. 11A**, **1995 McDonald's South / White Jerseys All-Star Football Team** lists **No. 19 Michael Bishop**, **Class 4A**, **quarterback**, **6 ft 2 in**, **200 lb**, **Willis High School**, **Blinn Junior College** as college destination. This establishes a published all-star roster selection, not verified participation or game statistics. [Original 1995 newspaper roster](https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth790648/m1/11/).

## 1998 Big 12 Championship — documented pass

- **December 5, 1998:** In the final play of regulation against Texas A&M, Kansas State quarterback **Michael Bishop** threw a Hail Mary pass that was caught by **Everett Burnett** near the goal line; **Toya Jones** stopped Burnett short of a touchdown. Texas A&M later won **36–33 in double overtime**. [Texas A&M Athletics original game account](https://12thman.com/news/1998/12/05/10-texas-am-36-1-kansas-state-33).
- **Source limitation:** The 1995 all-star listing and 1998 college game are different events. Do not infer an all-star appearance or a specific full-game passing line from either source. The receiving/tackle spot is reported differently by contemporary accounts; see [Everett Burnett](../everett-burnett/MASTER-RECORD.md).
