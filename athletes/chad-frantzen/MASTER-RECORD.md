# Chad Frantzen — A1 permanent individual research record

## Documented historical football evidence

Sophomore Texas A&M football player from Diana High School, named the **12th Man MVP** at the banquet following the 1997 season. The university reports **seven tackles on kickoffs** during that season.

**Original source:** Texas A&M University Athletics, “Football Award Banquet Honorees,” published **February 2, 1998**: https://12thman.com/news/1998/02/02/football-award-banquet-honorees . The banquet followed the 1997 football season; do not misdate the award as a separate 1998 season achievement.
