Drafted by Chicago in **round 2, No. 32 overall** in the 1990 NFL draft, from **Texas Christian University**. - Born July 11, 1967; died December 21, 1990. Preserve the distinction from **Fred Earl Washington Sr.**, a separate football player. [Pro Football Reference](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/W/WashFr21.htm).
