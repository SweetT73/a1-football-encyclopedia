# Branndon Stewart — A1 permanent individual research record

## Documented historical football evidence

Junior Texas A&M quarterback from Stephenville High School, named **offensive MVP** following the 1997 football season. The university reports **56.6% completion rate, 10 touchdown passes and 4 interceptions** for the season and credits him with leading a **28–25 overtime win over Oklahoma State** and the Big 12 South Division championship.

**Original source:** Texas A&M University Athletics, “Football Award Banquet Honorees,” published **February 2, 1998**: https://12thman.com/news/1998/02/02/football-award-banquet-honorees . The banquet followed the 1997 football season; do not misdate the award as a separate 1998 season achievement.
