# Matthew Rutledge "Matt" Schaub — A1 permanent individual record

## Identification
- **Name:** Matthew Rutledge Schaub; commonly Matt Schaub.
- **Sport / position:** American football quarterback.
- **College:** University of Virginia, 2000–2003.
- **NFL:** Atlanta Falcons, Houston Texans, Oakland Raiders, Baltimore Ravens, and later Atlanta Falcons; distinguish professional and collegiate records.
- **Prior A1 conversation provenance:** Matt Schaub research/video sources were mentioned in earlier A1 conversations but were not yet confirmed as permanently transferred. This record preserves researched facts, **not** video bytes or original photos.

## Verified college career
The University of Virginia's official passing-records publication lists Schaub's 2000–2003 career as **716 completions in 1,069 attempts, 7,502 passing yards, 56 passing touchdowns and 26 interceptions**. The 2002 season finished with **2,976 passing yards and 28 passing touchdowns**; the 2003 season with **2,952 passing yards and 18 passing touchdowns**. [UVA official passing records](https://virginiasports.com/news/2010/08/16/uva-records-passing); [Virginia 2025 official football record book](https://static.virginiasports.com/custompages/sports/m-footbl/factbook/2025/2025_Virginia_Football_Record_Book_web.pdf).

### 2002 individual game evidence
- **At Wake Forest, September 2002:** Virginia's original September 30, 2002 release credits Schaub with **22 completions in 27 attempts, 312 passing yards, three passing touchdowns**, and **two rushes for six yards** in a 38–34 Virginia win. He was named ACC Offensive Back of the Week. [UVA contemporary release, September 30, 2002](https://virginiasports.com/news/2002/09/30/matt-schaub-named-acc-player-of-the-week).
- **Versus Maryland, November 2002:** UVA's contemporary ACC honors release credits Schaub with **23 completions in 27 attempts, 249 passing yards and three passing touchdowns** in the upset of No. 18 Maryland. [UVA release dated November 27, 2002](https://virginiasports.com/news/2002/11/27/virginia-s-matt-schaub-named-acc-offensive-amp-player-of-the-year).

### 2002 awards
- **ACC Player of the Year** and **ACC Offensive Player of the Year**, announced November 26–27, 2002 in voting by the Atlantic Coast Sports Media Association for the Associated Press; also first-team All-ACC quarterback. [UVA contemporary award announcement](https://virginiasports.com/news/2002/11/27/virginia-s-matt-schaub-named-acc-offensive-amp-player-of-the-year).
- **Important date-specific distinction:** UVA's November 27, 2002 report gives **2,751 yards and 26 TD passes at the time of the award**. Its later full-season record book gives **2,976 yards and 28 TD passes**. These figures reflect different reporting dates; do not treat them as contradictory careers or add them together.

## Verified NFL career
The NFL's official career table lists **155 regular-season games**, **2,148 completions in 3,348 attempts**, **25,467 passing yards**, **136 passing touchdowns**, and **91 interceptions** across 2004–2020. [NFL official career stats](https://www.nfl.com/players/matt-schaub/stats/career). His **2009 Houston Texans** passing line is **4,770 yards and 29 touchdowns** in 16 games, according to the same table.
