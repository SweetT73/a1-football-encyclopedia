# John William Heisman — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** John William Heisman (October 23, 1869 – October 3, 1936), American football player, coach and innovator; namesake of the Heisman Memorial Trophy. A1 covers him in its coaching encyclopedia.

## Existing A1 research, preserved with source distinctions

- **Playing:** University of Pennsylvania football; the existing illustrated A1 profile includes an 1890 Penn team photograph with a source caption identifying Heisman as second row, second from right.
- **Early coaching:** Oberlin (1892), Buchtel (1893), Oberlin (1894), Auburn (1895–1899), Clemson (1900–1903).
- **Clemson, 1903:** The existing A1 profile describes a 4–1–1 season and a shared Southern Intercollegiate Athletic Association title.
- **Georgia Tech, 1904–1919:** The existing A1 profile records the 1916 222–0 Cumberland game and 1917 national championship claim. Avoid attributing all early football rules innovations to Heisman alone.
- **Later coaching:** Pennsylvania (1920–1922), Washington & Jefferson (1923), Rice (1924–1927).
- **Honors:** College Football Hall of Fame induction, 1954, per existing A1 source. The Downtown Athletic Club award was renamed in his memory after his 1936 death.
- **Reported career record:** 186–70–18 in the existing A1 profile; reconcile against original institutional records before treating as a final verified count.
