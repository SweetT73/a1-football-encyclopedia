## Career identity and historical anchors

Walter Payton is one A1 identity spanning Jackson State, the Chicago Bears, historical Madden/EA representations, and later Legends/Ultimate Legends items. His TRUE family is halfback/running back only. Fullback and wide-receiver secondary-position OVRs and all quarterback items are excluded from the TRUE average.

- 13 Chicago seasons and 178 consecutive starts including postseason.
- Retired as the NFL leader in carries (3,838), rushing yards (16,726), rushing touchdowns (110), running-back receptions (492), running-back receiving yards (4,538), and scrimmage yards (21,264).
- Nine Pro Bowls; seven All-Pro selections; 1977 NFL MVP and Offensive Player of the Year.
- Super Bowl XX champion; NFL 1970s and 1980s All-Decade teams; NFL 100 All-Time Team.
- Passing résumé: 34 attempts, 11 completions, 331 yards, eight touchdowns, and six interceptions. This supports his versatility identity but does not permit QB-family ratings to enter the TRUE HB average.
