# Wayne Pate — A1 permanent individual record

**A1 role:** Track-and-field coach directly associated with James Lott's collegiate high-jump career at the University of Texas. This is a coach's individual record; do not mislabel him as a Refugio football player.

## Verified coaching and athletic history

- **University of Texas:** Indiana University Athletics' July 15, 1997 staff announcement says Wayne Pate spent **seven years as a Texas assistant track-and-field coach**, where he coached **three-time NCAA high-jump champion James Lott** and **two-time NCAA long-jump champion Eric Metcalf**. This is institutional evidence for Pate's coaching relationship, not a claim that Pate won Lott's individual titles.
- **Indiana University athlete:** Indiana's announcement says Pate graduated in **1981** with a physical-education degree; was a **two-time Big Ten long-jump champion**; and competed in the high jump, 100 meters and 4×100-meter relay.
- **Coaching appointments as reported July 15, 1997:** Four years as assistant at Tennessee; seven years as assistant at Texas; three years as Murray State head men's and women's track/cross-country coach; three seasons at Purdue; newly named Indiana assistant track-and-field coach.
- **Primary source:** [Indiana University Athletics, “Indiana University Track & Field Coaching Staff Announcements,” July 15, 1997](https://iuhoosiers.com/news/1997/7/15/INDIANA_UNIVERSITY_TRACK_amp_FIELD_COACHING_STAFF_ANNOUNCEMENTS).
