# Ted Nelson — A1 permanent individual record

## Contemporary Texas A&M coaching evidence
- On **May 7, 1999**, *The Battalion* identified **Ted Nelson as Texas A&M's track and field coach** and quoted his discussion of the men's 4×100-meter relay team featuring Toya Cardin Jones, Billy Fobbs, Michael Price and freshman Brandon Evans.
- Nelson said the new lead-off assignment for Evans was designed to reduce baton-exchange difficulties. The report also described his goals for the upcoming Big 12 and NCAA championships; these were **goals**, not documented results.
- The same page's preview of the May 8 home meet identifies Nelson as **head track and field coach**.

## Original source
- [Tom Kennedy, “Changing of the guard” and May 8 meet preview, *The Battalion*, May 7, 1999, p. 13](https://newspaper.library.tamu.edu/lccn/sn86088544/1999-05-07/ed-1/seq-13.pdf).

## Archive safeguards
- Record is a **coach's individual profile**, not evidence that Nelson competed in any named event. His coaching tenure outside May 1999 is not established by this source alone.
- No authenticated individual portrait has been filed; no conversation is declared cleared.
