# Garland Arthur “Gardie” Grange — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Garland Arthur Grange (December 2, 1906 – May 28, 1981), also known as Gardie or Pinkey Grange. Younger brother of Harold “Red” Grange; a distinct athlete with his own college and professional career.

## Career evidence preserved from existing A1 research

- **Early life and school:** Born in Forksville, Pennsylvania. The existing A1 Garland Grange illustrated profile reports graduation from Wheaton High School in 1924 and Illinois freshman football.
- **University of Illinois:** The A1 profile reports a shoulder injury preventing varsity participation in 1925–1926; a 1927 Illinois 7–0 championship season, wearing No. 22.
- **Chicago Bears, 1929–1931:** The A1 profile lists Garland as a professional football end, wearing No. 25. His playing years must not be merged into Red Grange's record.
- **1932:** The existing profile lists the St. Louis Veterans (player-coach) and Memphis Tigers; exact game participation, league designation, and team chronology still require original-source verification.
- **Later life:** The A1 profile reports work in Miami, including the credit department of Richards Department Store, and high-school football officiating. It records his death in Miami on May 28, 1981.

These are facts **reported in the user's existing A1 research document**. They are not represented as newly independently inspected original game records.
