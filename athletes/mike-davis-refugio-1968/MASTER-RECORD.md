# Mike Davis — Refugio 1968 — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Mike Davis, quarterback on the **1968 Refugio High School** football team in Texas. This school/year-qualified folder avoids assigning the statistics to unrelated football players with the same name.

## Contemporary November 29, 1968 game

The *Refugio Timely Remarks* of **December 5, 1968, p. 8**, reports Refugio's **21–19** victory over Belton. After Refugio took possession with approximately **1:01** remaining, the article specifically identifies quarterback **Mike Davis** as taking the snap and **kneeling on three plays** to finish the game. Do not treat the three kneels as an individual rushing-yardage line without a game box score.

Preserve the printed numerator and denominator and flag the percentage for original-page verification; do not silently correct the newspaper.
