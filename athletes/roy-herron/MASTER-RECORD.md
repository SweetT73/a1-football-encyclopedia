# Roy Herron — A1 permanent individual record

**Documented Refugio association:** The existing A1 Toya Cardin Jones historical page names **Roy Herron** as a member of Refugio's **1993 UIL state 4×100-meter relay** alongside **Pete Perez, Dana Robinson, and Toya Jones**. The page reports **first place** with 41.3 seconds in the original state-meet account and 41.44 in a later UIL record listing; these are reporting conventions for the same relay, not separate performances.

**Source:** [Toya Cardin Jones A1 high-school track record](../toya-cardin-jones/index.html). Original meet sheet and Roy Herron's individual leg/order need independent verification. Do not infer other individual results or identify Roy in an unidentified group photograph.

**Photographs:** No independently identified original portrait has been located. The Refugio group photograph [12317.png](../toya-cardin-jones/12317.png) does not identify the people individually; no duplicate file has been made.

## 1992 Refugio football — separately documented All-State awards

- **1992 Associated Press Sports Editors Class 2A FIRST-TEAM defensive back**, Refugio High School. The historical Texas Sports Writers compilation prints **“Herron, Roy”**, senior, **185 lb**.
- The **same 1992 APSE Class 2A selection** separately names **Roy Herron, Refugio, as Defensive Player of the Year**. This is a distinct individual honor in the same season, not a second first-team berth or a 1993 award.
- These football honors precede his separately preserved **1993 Refugio 4 × 100 m relay** association above. They do not establish a particular football game, start, tackle or interception.

**Source:** Billy Wilbanks, Texas Sports Writers Association, historical All-State football compilation (2010), 1981–1995 volume, **1992 APSE Class 2A first-team defense and Defensive Player of the Year entries**: https://txswa.org/All-State%201921-2010/3-Football-1981-1995.pdf . The original 1992 AP newspaper publication remains to be located for direct confirmation.
