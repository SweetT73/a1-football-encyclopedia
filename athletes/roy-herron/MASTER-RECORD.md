# Roy Herron — A1 permanent individual record

**Documented Refugio association:** The existing A1 Toya Cardin Jones historical page names **Roy Herron** as a member of Refugio's **1993 UIL state 4×100-meter relay** alongside **Pete Perez, Dana Robinson, and Toya Jones**. The page reports **first place** with 41.3 seconds in the original state-meet account and 41.44 in a later UIL record listing; these are reporting conventions for the same relay, not separate performances.

**Source:** [Toya Cardin Jones A1 high-school track record](../toya-cardin-jones/index.html). Original meet sheet and Roy Herron's individual leg/order need independent verification. Do not infer other individual results or identify Roy in an unidentified group photograph.

**Photographs:** No independently identified original portrait has been located. The Refugio group photograph [12317.png](../toya-cardin-jones/12317.png) does not identify the people individually; no duplicate file has been made.
