# Bill Wade — A1 permanent individual record

## Football history and championship context

- Led the **Chicago Bears to the 1963 NFL Championship**, a **14–10 victory over the New York Giants**. The source's identification of Wade as the championship quarterback is preserved without inferring additional game statistics.
- Bears quarterback **Jim McMahon** later led Chicago to its 1985-season **Super Bowl XX** victory, **46–10 over the New England Patriots, January 26, 1986**. This is contextual comparison, not an additional Wade achievement.
- **Mike Ditka** was Wade's teammate on the **1963 Bears championship team**.
