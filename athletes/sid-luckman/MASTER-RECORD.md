# Sid Luckman — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Sidney “Sid” Luckman, Chicago Bears quarterback, **No. 42**, member of the Pro Football Hall of Fame **Class of 1965**. [Pro Football Hall of Fame player biography](https://www.profootballhof.com/players/sid-luckman).

## Verified football record

- **Chicago Bears quarterback for 12 NFL seasons**; Pro Football Hall of Fame credits **14,686 career passing yards, 137 passing touchdowns, and five All-NFL selections**.
- Key figure in the Bears' T-formation offense under George Halas. His Bears won **four NFL championships** during his career, in **1940, 1941, 1943 and 1946**. [Pro Football Hall of Fame biography](https://www.profootballhof.com/players/sid-luckman); [Chicago Bears championship history](https://www.chicagobears.com/history/).
- Named **NFL Most Valuable Player in 1943**. The Hall of Fame records his **seven touchdown passes against the New York Giants on November 14, 1943** and **five touchdown passes in the 1943 championship game**. [Hall of Fame](https://www.profootballhof.com/players/sid-luckman).
- The Bears retired **No. 42**. [Pro Football Hall of Fame, Chicago Bears team facts](https://www.profootballhof.com/teams/chicago-bears/team-facts).
