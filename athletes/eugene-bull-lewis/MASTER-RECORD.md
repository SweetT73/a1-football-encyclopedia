# Eugene “Bull” Lewis — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Eugene “Bull” Lewis, Refugio High School (Texas) quarterback/halfback, runner, passer and kicker in **1968**. **Do not conflate him with Calvin Lewis**, the separately named and still-unidentified A1 subject, or with Edward Lewis, also listed on the 1968 team.

## Contemporary game report — Refugio 21, Belton 19

The *Refugio Timely Remarks* of **December 5, 1968, p. 8**, describes Refugio's **November 29, 1968** bi-district victory over Belton in San Marcos:
- Lewis completed a **12-yard touchdown pass to Gary Lott** late in the first half.
- He ran **32 yards for a third-quarter touchdown**; the same drive also credits him with runs of **6 and 9 yards** and completions to Lott and Bryan Shaw.
- The account describes Lewis's late passing during the winning drive, including a pass to Efren “DeDe” Gipson for the decisive touchdown, with Gary Lott delivering a block.
- The reporter credits Lewis with **three successful extra-point kicks** in the 21–19 win.

## 1968 season-to-date figures published December 5

The newspaper's tables summarize **12 games**, not merely the Belton game:
- **Rushing:** 169 carries, **1,320 yards**, **14 rushing touchdowns**; printed average **7.81 yards/carry**.
- **Passing:** 72 attempts, **38 completions**, **654 yards**, **6 passing touchdowns**; printed completion percentage **52.78%**.
- **Punting:** **39 punts**, **35.28-yard average**.
- **Placekicking:** **2 of 3 field goals**, **34 of 39 extra-point kicks**; additionally **six successful two-point conversion runs**, as separately described by the newspaper.
- **Scoring:** **166 points** in the printed individual scoring table.

**Original source:** *Refugio Timely Remarks*, vol. 51, no. 12, Thursday, December 5, 1968, p. 8, “Bobcat Rally Wins Belton Game 21-19” and the adjacent cumulative statistical tables: https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth651690/m1/8/ .

## Evidence limits

The name **Eugene “Bull” Lewis** is explicit in the contemporary article. The source does not establish any relationship or identity connection to **Calvin Lewis**, and these statistics must not be moved to Calvin's record. Numerical entries should be checked against the original page image if a later source conflicts with OCR.
