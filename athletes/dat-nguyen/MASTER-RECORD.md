# Dat Nguyen — A1 permanent individual research record

## Documented historical football evidence

Junior Texas A&M linebacker from Rockport-Fulton High School; the university named him the **Wrecking Crew MVP**. Its 1997 season summary credits **130 tackles, 3 forced fumbles, 4 fumble recoveries, 3 pass breakups, and an interception returned 32 yards for a touchdown**. It also credits him with another touchdown on a fumble return. In the **1998 Cotton Bowl**, the university reports **20 tackles (15 solo, 5 assisted)**, one sack for six yards lost, and an interception that he lateraled to Brandon Jennings during an **83-yard return touchdown**. Do not attribute all 83 return yards to Nguyen.

**Original source:** Texas A&M University Athletics, “Football Award Banquet Honorees,” published **February 2, 1998**: https://12thman.com/news/1998/02/02/football-award-banquet-honorees . The banquet followed the 1997 football season; do not misdate the award as a separate 1998 season achievement.
