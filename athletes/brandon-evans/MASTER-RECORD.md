# Brandon Evans — A1 permanent individual record

## Documented association
- **Texas A&M track and field, May 1999:** A contemporary *The Battalion* report dated **May 7, 1999, page 13** identifies **Brandon Evans as the planned first-leg runner** in a Texas A&M men's 4×100-meter relay lineup: Brandon Evans (first), Toya Cardin Jones (second), Billy Fobbs (third), Michael Price (fourth).
- This is **a reported planned running order**, not proof the relay raced in that order or proof of an official result. Do not infer any individual medal, season statistics, or performance from it.

## Additional contemporary details — original article checked
- The same May 7, 1999 article identifies Evans as a **freshman** newly selected by Texas A&M coach **Ted Nelson** for the lead-off leg after a search for a fourth relay member. Nelson described the first-leg choice as a way to reduce handoff difficulties.
