# Steve McKinney — A1 permanent individual research record

## Documented historical football evidence

Senior Texas A&M offensive lineman from Clear Lake High School, selected as the **offensive captain** at the annual football banquet following the 1997 season. The university describes him as a **consensus All-Big 12** selection and reports **34 consecutive career starts at left guard**.

**Original source:** Texas A&M University Athletics, “Football Award Banquet Honorees,” published **February 2, 1998**: https://12thman.com/news/1998/02/02/football-award-banquet-honorees . The banquet followed the 1997 football season; do not misdate the award as a separate 1998 season achievement.
