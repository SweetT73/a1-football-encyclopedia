# Ragan Dunham — A1 permanent individual record

## Verified 1993 Refugio High School football honor and statistics

- **Name as printed:** Ragan Dunham.
- **School:** Refugio High School, Refugio, Texas.
- **1993 Associated Press Sports Editors Class 2A All-State:** **FIRST-TEAM defensive back**.
- **Published class / measurements:** Senior; **5 ft 9 in, 170 lb**. These are contemporary roster listings, not independently measured dimensions.
- **Published regular-season defensive statistics:** **8 interceptions and 85 tackles**. The AP introduction expressly says the All-State selections were based on regular-season statistics. Do **not** assign these totals to any one game, to postseason play, or to Toya Jones.

**Original contemporary source:** *Big Spring Herald*, Thursday **December 16, 1993**, sports section, page **9** (PDF page 9), Associated Press Sports Editors Class 2A All-State team, first-team defensive-back listing. [Original scanned newspaper](https://newspapers.swco.ttu.edu/bitstream/handle/20.500.12255/296053/Big_Spring_Herald_1993_12_16.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1#page=9).

## Relationship to other Refugio evidence
The same original report lists **Toya Jones, Refugio junior, 6 ft 2 in, 190 lb**, on the **second-team defensive-back** roster. Dunham's first-team honor and his 8 interceptions / 85 tackles are **his own**, not Jones's. This separation is critical for the A1 individual encyclopedia.
