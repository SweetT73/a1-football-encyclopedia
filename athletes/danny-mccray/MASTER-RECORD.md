# Danny McCray — A1 permanent individual record

## Documented Texas A&M track career
- Contemporary *The Battalion* reporting dated **May 7, 1999, p. 13** names **Danny McCray** as the fourth member, alongside Billy Fobbs, Michael Price and Toya Cardin Jones, of Texas A&M's **1997 national-championship 4×100-meter relay**. The article describes McCray as a **senior in 1997** and as having departed the team before the 1999 season.
- The newspaper reports that *Track & Field News* ranked McCray **10th in the world in the 400-meter dash** at the time of the May 1999 report. This is a **secondhand contemporaneous ranking claim**, not an independently checked Track & Field News ranking table.
- The 1999 team sought a replacement lead-off runner, ultimately selecting freshman Brandon Evans. Do not infer McCray's 1997 relay leg from the 1999 lineup.
