# Gary Lott — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Gary Lott, Refugio High School (Texas) halfback on the **1968** football team. **Distinct from James Lott**, the Refugio player whose documented high-school career culminated in 1982. No familial relationship is established by the cited evidence.

## Original contemporary newspaper: Refugio versus Belton, November 29, 1968

The *Refugio Timely Remarks*, **Thursday, December 5, 1968, p. 8**, reports Refugio's **21–19** bi-district victory over Belton at Evans Field in San Marcos the preceding Friday. The article explicitly credits Gary Lott with:
- Catching a **12-yard touchdown pass** from Eugene “Bull” Lewis during a first-half comeback.
- Catching a **31-yard pass** from Lewis on a third-quarter scoring drive; the same drive included a **1-yard loss** on another Lewis-to-Lott completion.
- Catching passes of **6 and 5 yards** during Refugio's decisive fourth-quarter drive.
- Delivering a key block on Belton's defender during Efren “DeDe” Gipson's late go-ahead touchdown reception. The reporter describes the block; do not convert it into a defensive tackle.

## Season-to-date statistics printed in the December 5 article

These are **1968 cumulative figures through 12 games**, not a box score for the single Belton game:
- **Rushing:** 128 carries, **949 yards**, **7 rushing touchdowns** (printed average 7.41 yards/carry).
- **Passing:** 33 attempts, **11 completions**, **229 yards**, **4 passing touchdowns** (printed completion percentage 33.33%).
- **Receiving:** **42 catches**, **735 yards**, **13 receiving touchdowns** (printed average 17.50 yards/catch).
- **Scoring:** **144 points** in the newspaper's scoring table, combining multiple scoring categories.
