# Brandon Jennings — A1 permanent individual research record

## Documented historical football evidence

Texas A&M football player credited in the original university banquet report as receiving **Dat Nguyen's lateral** after Nguyen intercepted a pass in the **1998 Cotton Bowl**, completing an **83-yard interception-return touchdown**. The source does not allocate the 83 yards between the players, so do not claim Jennings individually ran 83 yards.

**Original source:** Texas A&M University Athletics, “Football Award Banquet Honorees,” published **February 2, 1998**: https://12thman.com/news/1998/02/02/football-award-banquet-honorees . The report also documents Toya Cardin Jones's special-teams captain selection.

## Provenance safeguards

- Do not attribute this entire return to either Jennings or Nguyen alone; the university describes a lateral.
- No original photograph was inspected or transferred for Jennings.
- No source conversation is certified fully archived or safe for deletion.
