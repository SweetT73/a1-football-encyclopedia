# Billy Fobbs — A1 permanent individual record

## Documented association
- **Texas A&M track and field, May 1999:** A contemporary *The Battalion* report dated **May 7, 1999, page 13** identifies **Billy Fobbs as the planned third-leg runner** in a Texas A&M men's 4×100-meter relay lineup: Brandon Evans (first), Toya Cardin Jones (second), Billy Fobbs (third), Michael Price (fourth).
- The newspaper describes a **planned relay order**, not an official completed race result. No medal, individual split, race appearance, or season record is inferred.

## Verified contemporary career evidence — May 7, 1999
- *The Battalion* identifies Billy Fobbs as a **Texas A&M senior**, **two-time All-American**, and the school's then-**No. 4 all-time 100-meter performer**, with a reported personal best of **10.08 seconds**. This is a contemporary report, not independently reproduced timing-sheet evidence.
- The article says he held multiple Southwest Conference and Big 12 championships, but **does not enumerate events or years**; do not assign specific individual titles without meet records.
- The reporter says Fobbs missed competition in **1998 because of injury** and describes a later hamstring problem. These are historical reported circumstances, not a verified medical record.
- The article identifies Fobbs, Michael Price, Toya Jones and Danny McCray as members of Texas A&M's **1997 national-champion 4×100-meter relay**. This is a contemporary team claim; original NCAA final results should be checked before adding exact legs or times.
- [Tom Kennedy, “Changing of the guard,” *The Battalion*, May 7, 1999, p. 13](https://newspaper.library.tamu.edu/lccn/sn86088544/1999-05-07/ed-1/seq-13.pdf).


## 1997 NCAA men's 4 × 100 m relay — exact university-record-book result

- **June 6, 1997, Bloomington, Indiana — NCAA final:** Texas A&M's published historical track-and-field performance register lists **Michael Price, Toya Jones, Billy Fobbs and Danny McCray** as the Aggies' men's **4 × 100 m relay team**, with an official listed performance of **38.80 seconds**, annotated **“1) NCAA Final”**. [Texas A&M Athletics historical track performance register, PDF](https://12thman.com/documents/download/2015/6/30/1.pdf).
- **Corroboration:** Tom Kennedy's contemporary *The Battalion* report, **May 7, 1999, p. 13**, independently identifies the same four men as members of the **1997 national-championship relay**. [Original 1999 newspaper](https://newspaper.library.tamu.edu/lccn/sn86088544/1999-05-07/ed-1/seq-13.pdf).
- **Scope and uncertainty:** The 38.80 time and first-place NCAA final result are from a **later university historical record book**, not an original 1997 timing sheet. The two sources do not specify each runner's **1997 relay leg or individual split**. Do not transfer the **planned 1999 lineup order** into the 1997 championship, or count this team title as an individual sprint championship or UIL medal.
