# Shawn Lewis — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Shawn Lewis, Woodsboro High School boys track athlete. Do not confuse him with **Calvin Lewis**, whose identity and sports history are separately unresolved.

## Contemporary results — May 1992
- **May 16, 1992, UIL Class 2A boys state meet, Austin:** second place in the **300-meter hurdles, 37.8 seconds**. [Big Spring Herald, May 17, 1992, p. 5-B (PDF p. 13)](https://newspapers.swco.ttu.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/ac5e87a7-eb70-4724-8f6f-8b2599513eeb/content#page=13). This is a reported result, not a verified official meet-sheet time.
- **Woodsboro 400-meter relay:** first, **42.2 seconds**, with the surnames **Friar, Nelsoney, Anderson, Lewis** in the contemporary report. Sweet T corrected the surname spellings. The list does not prove leg order or individual splits.
- **Woodsboro 1,600-meter relay:** first, **3:18.0**, with surnames **Anderson, Nelsoney, Valderrama, Lewis**. Again, no verified leg order.
- Woodsboro boys won the state team championship with **61 points**. Team result is context, not an additional individual championship.
