# Efren “DeDe” Gipson — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Efren Gipson, also called **“DeDe” Gipson** in the contemporary report, Refugio High School (Texas) football halfback on the **1968** team. The nickname and formal first name appear in the same December 5 newspaper page and refer to this athlete.

## Original 1968 game reporting

The *Refugio Timely Remarks*, **December 5, 1968, p. 8**, describes Refugio's **21–19** victory over Belton on the preceding Friday, **November 29**. In the fourth quarter, the article credits Gipson with catching a pass from **Eugene “Bull” Lewis** and running for the **go-ahead touchdown**, with a key block from Gary Lott. The OCR is incomplete at the passage giving the precise play yardage; **do not invent a reception distance**. In Belton's last possession, the reporter names **Raymond Anderson and DeDe Gipson** as the defenders who stopped Robert Ford for a one-yard gain on a decisive play.

## 1968 cumulative statistics in the same newspaper

The statistical tables cover **12 games through the date of publication**, not only the Belton game:
- **Rushing:** **94 carries**, **782 yards**, **10 rushing touchdowns**, printed average **8.32 yards/carry**.
- **Receiving:** **27 receptions**, **421 yards**, **3 receiving touchdowns**, printed average **15.59 yards/catch**.
- **Scoring:** **100 points** in the article's individual scoring table.

**Source:** *Refugio Timely Remarks*, vol. 51, no. 12, December 5, 1968, page 8, “Bobcat Rally Wins Belton Game 21-19” and accompanying season tables: https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth651690/m1/8/ .

## Evidence and preservation

This is a distinct 1968 Refugio athlete, not James Lott or any later Refugio player. The printed page should resolve any OCR ambiguities before further precision is asserted. No authentic individual photograph was obtained from the source, and no conversation is certified cleared.
