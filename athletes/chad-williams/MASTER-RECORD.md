# Chad Williams — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Chad Williams, Refugio High School (Texas) football player, listed as a senior offensive guard in the **1981 Class AAA** Texas Sports Writers Association All-State selections. This is the Refugio player in the 1981 published roster; do not merge with other people of the same name.

## Contemporary documented honor

- **1981 season — TSWA Class AAA FIRST-TEAM ALL-STATE, offensive guard (OG).** The published selection identifies **Chad Williams, Refugio, 6 ft 1 in, 210 lb, senior**. This is an individual award, not a team statistic.
- **Primary source:** *The Hondo Anvil Herald*, January 14, 1982, p. 2, Section 2 (digitized page 12), “Rowdy Moos makes All-State team,” full Class AAA All-State selections. [Original scanned newspaper and transcription](https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth817368/m1/12/).
- **A1 relationship:** The same original 1981 roster is already cited in [James Lott's permanent record](../james-lott/MASTER-RECORD.md). Its 1981 honors belong to Chad Williams, not to Lott.
