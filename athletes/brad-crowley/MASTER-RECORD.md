# Brad Crowley — A1 permanent individual research record

## Documented historical football evidence

Senior Texas A&M defensive end from Corpus Christi Calallen High School. At the team's annual football awards banquet, he received the **Aggie Heart Award** and was named **defensive team captain**. The university reported that he started the final **16 games** of his career and had **67 tackles**, including **15 behind the line of scrimmage**. These are the university's stated career/season context; do not assign undocumented dates to the statistics.

**Original source:** Texas A&M University Athletics, “Football Award Banquet Honorees,” published **February 2, 1998**: https://12thman.com/news/1998/02/02/football-award-banquet-honorees . The banquet followed the 1997 football season; do not misdate the award as a separate 1998 season achievement.
