# Raymond Anderson — Refugio 1968 — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Raymond Anderson, Refugio High School (Texas) **1968** football fullback. **Separate from the existing 1992 Woodsboro Raymond Anderson track record and Ray Anderson football record**; the 1968 source does not establish that any of those later athletes are the same man. This date-and-school-qualified permanent folder prevents accidental merging.

## Original November 29, 1968 game evidence

The *Refugio Timely Remarks*, **December 5, 1968, p. 8**, reports Refugio's **21–19** win over Belton. In the final Belton possession, it credits **Raymond Anderson and Efren “DeDe” Gipson** with stopping Belton's Robert Ford for a **one-yard gain**, after which Refugio took possession and ran out the clock. The reporter names both defenders; do not assign the play solely to either.

## 1968 season-to-date statistics printed December 5

The 12-game cumulative rushing table lists **Raymond Anderson: 50 carries, 271 rushing yards, 7 rushing touchdowns, average 5.42 yards/carry**. The scoring table lists **42 points**. These are season-to-date figures, not a Belton-only box score.

**Primary source:** *Refugio Timely Remarks*, vol. 51, no. 12, Thursday, December 5, 1968, page 8, “Bobcat Rally Wins Belton Game 21-19” and the individual rushing/scoring tables: https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth651690/m1/8/ .
