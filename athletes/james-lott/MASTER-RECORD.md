# James Lott — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity and scope.** James Lott, Refugio, Texas, high-school football and track-and-field athlete; University of Texas football defensive back and high jumper. "James live" is a voice-to-text variant used in Sweet T's A1 conversations, not a separate person.

## Verified historical achievements

- **1983 Refugio High School state track meet:** 34.5 individual points, with high jump **1st, 7 ft 4¾ in**; long jump **1st, 24 ft 1 in**; high hurdles **2nd, 13.7 seconds**; 300-meter hurdles **3rd, 37.4 seconds**; and participation on the sixth-place 1600-meter relay team. The University of Texas Hall of Honor biography attributes the state team title to his performance. [UT Athletics](https://texaslonghorns.com/honors/hall-of-honor/james-lott/886/kiosk).
- **University of Texas football, 1983–1985:** starter at cornerback and safety. The UT biography lists **1984: 11 games, 20 tackles, 13 pass breakups** (team-leading); **1985: 11 games, 30 tackles, 7 pass breakups**. [UT Athletics](https://texaslonghorns.com/honors/hall-of-honor/james-lott/886/kiosk).
- **NCAA high jump championships:** 1986 indoor and outdoor, and 1987 indoor — three national titles; four-time All-American, three Southwest Conference high-jump titles as enumerated by UT Athletics. [UT Athletics](https://texaslonghorns.com/honors/hall-of-honor/james-lott/886/kiosk).
- **1988 U.S. Olympic Trials high jump final, July 16:** **4th place, 2.29 m (7 ft 6 in)**. Official published affiliation in A1 user-facing format: **Unattached — Refugio**. This is the exact affiliation Sweet T requested for his archive. [USATF original results, p. 11](https://www.usatf.org/USATF/media/USATF/1988-Olympic-Trials-agate-results.pdf). The original result prints "Unattached, Refugio, Texas".
- **Honors:** Texas High School Football Hall of Fame (1994); University of Texas Hall of Honor (2017). [UT Athletics](https://texaslonghorns.com/honors/hall-of-honor/james-lott/886/kiosk).
