# James Lott — 1983 regional meet record evidence

Texas A&M–Kingsville's April 26, 2023 *Javelina Highlights* historical Region IV-4A meet records list James Lott of Refugio with the following **1983 regional-meet marks**:

- Boys high jump: **7 ft 0¼ in**.
- Boys long jump: **24 ft 4½ in**.

These regional-meet marks are **not** the 1983 UIL state final marks of high jump 7 ft 4¾ in and long jump 24 ft 1 in. The historical record-book's modern Region IV-4A heading does not establish Lott's high-school classification in 1983. The original event date and jump attempts are not specified.

Source: Texas A&M–Kingsville, *Javelina Highlights*, April 26, 2023, p. 11: https://s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/sidearm.nextgen.sites/tamuk.sidearmsports.com/documents/2023/4/26/highlights875.pmd.pdf .
