# Everett Burnett — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity and scope:** Everett Burnett, Kansas State football receiver in the **December 5, 1998 Big 12 Championship Game** against Texas A&M. This is an original-game-evidence entry, not a complete career biography.

## Documented individual game play

- **Final play of regulation, December 5, 1998:** Kansas State quarterback **Michael Bishop** threw a Hail Mary that was batted around and caught by **Everett Burnett** near the Texas A&M goal line; **Toya Jones tackled/drove Burnett backward**, preventing the touchdown. Texas A&M eventually defeated Kansas State **36–33 in double overtime**. [Texas A&M Athletics original game report](https://12thman.com/news/1998/12/05/10-texas-am-36-1-kansas-state-33).
- **Contemporary source discrepancy:** Texas A&M reports Burnett caught the ball at the **1-yard line**; the *Washington Post* (December 6, 1998) reports he was tackled by Jones at the **3-yard line**. These may refer to different moments (catch versus tackle), but that interpretation is **not proven**. Preserve both reported spots. [Washington Post original report](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/sports/1998/12/06/ucla-kansas-st-bowled-over/b1ca29f1-bf4b-430d-b9a4-454c158cab70/).
- **Safeguard:** The completion was **not a touchdown**. Do not invent additional receptions, yards, or game statistics from this single play.
