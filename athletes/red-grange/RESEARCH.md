# Madden 27-A1 — Harold Edward “Red” Grange — historical research

## Established player identity
- Full name: Harold Edward Grange
- Primary name: Red Grange
- Nicknames: **The Galloping Ghost**; **The Wheaton Iceman** (also “Ice Man” / “Wheaton Ice Man”). The Galloping Ghost is attributed to Chicago sportswriter Warren Brown. The ice-related name comes from his work delivering ice in Wheaton during college summers.
- Born: June 13, 1903, Forksville, Pennsylvania; died: January 28, 1991, Lake Wales, Florida.
- Position: Halfback (also played defensive back; a two-way player). A1 roster: HB, #77.
- Listed measurements: 6 ft 0 in, 180 lb (historical listed measurements, not Madden translated settings).
- College: University of Illinois, 1923–1925.

## Illinois accomplishments
- Three-time All-American, 1923–1925; 1924 unanimous All-American.
- 1923 national-championship Illinois team; 1924 Big Ten MVP / Chicago Tribune Silver Football.
- October 18, 1924 vs Michigan: returned opening kickoff 95 yards for a touchdown; scored three more touchdowns on runs of 67, 56, and 44 yards within the first 12 minutes; later added a rushing touchdown and threw a touchdown pass.
- Illinois retired his #77.

## Professional football
- Chicago Bears: 1925 and 1929–1934. New York Yankees: 1926–1927 (1926 AFL, 1927 NFL); did not play in 1928 after serious knee injury in 1927.
- Signed with Bears after final college game, professional debut Thanksgiving Day 1925 against Chicago Cardinals at Cubs Park. His celebrity and nationwide barnstorming tour helped promote the early NFL.
- Two NFL championships with Bears (1932, 1933); important touchdown catch in the 1932 playoff game and touchdown-saving defensive tackle in 1933 championship.
- Bears backfield coach, 1935–1937.
- NFL 1920s All-Decade Team; Chicago Bears retired #77.
- Pro Football Hall of Fame inaugural class of 1963; charter member of College Football Hall of Fame (1951).
