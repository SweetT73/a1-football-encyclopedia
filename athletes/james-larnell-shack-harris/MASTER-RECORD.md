# James Larnell "Shack" Harris — A1 Master Record

**Identity:** James Larnell Harris, nicknamed "Shack". **Position:** Quarterback (right-handed). **Jersey:** No. 12 with the Buffalo Bills, Los Angeles Rams and San Diego Chargers. **Date of birth:** July 20, **1947**, Monroe, Louisiana. **NOT 1949**. The *original 1975 Los Angeles Rams media guide*, page 36, explicitly reads "Born: 7-20-47"; also corroborated by Pro-Football-Reference. **High school:** Carroll High School, Monroe, Louisiana. **College:** Grambling State University. **Contemporary Rams measurements (1975):** 6 feet 4 inches, 210 pounds. 

## Football chronology
- **1965–1968:** Grambling State; coached by Eddie Robinson. Grambling MVP 1967 and 1968; led SWAC championship teams in both years; 1967 Orange Blossom Classic MVP (Rams official 1975 media guide).
- **1969:** Selected by Buffalo Bills, **8th round / 192nd overall**, NFL/AFL draft. Started Bills' opening game of 1969 AFL season, widely described as a historically important Black quarterback breakthrough; distinguish AFL season-opening achievement from NFL-specific history.
- **1969–1971:** Buffalo Bills active statistical seasons; released in 1972.
- **1973–1976:** Los Angeles Rams. No NFL game appearances in 1972.
- **1974:** Elevated to Rams starter; in first start vs. San Francisco 49ers, completed 12/15 passes for 276 yards and 3 TD, plus a rushing TD in 37–14 victory. Led the Rams to division title and **first NFL playoff victory by a Black starting QB** against Washington. Selected for 1974-season Pro Bowl, played in 1975, and awarded Pro Bowl MVP; be careful distinguishing the selection season from game calendar year.
- **1975:** Rams starting QB in 13 games, 2,148 passing yards and 14 passing TD.
- **1977–1979:** **San Diego** Chargers (NOT Los Angeles Chargers for those historical seasons).
- **Post-playing:** NFL personnel/executive roles including Tampa Bay, Jets, Ravens, Jaguars and Detroit. Member of Black College Football Hall of Fame, Southwestern Athletic Conference Hall of Fame, Grambling State Hall of Fame, Louisiana Sports Hall of Fame. The 2020 Rams retrospective says inducted into Black College Football Hall of Fame in 2012.

## NFL/AFL regular-season passing totals (Pro Football Reference and NFL.com)
- **83 games**, **607 completions on 1,149 attempts**, **52.8% completion**, **8,136 passing yards**, **45 touchdown passes**, **59 interceptions**, **67.3 passer rating**.
- Career rushing: **121 attempts, 367 yards, 10 touchdowns** (NFL.com).
- Pro Football Reference lists 41 starts and 25–16 record; an independent Pro Football Archives database lists 46 starts. **Do not merge these conflicting starts data as a verified single fact** until checking original starting lineup records.
