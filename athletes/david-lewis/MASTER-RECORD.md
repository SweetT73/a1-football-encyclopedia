# David Lewis — A1 permanent individual record

**School:** Refugio High School, Texas.

## Documented honor
- **1982 Texas Sports Writers Class 3A second-team All-State tight end.** Listed as "Lewis, David — Refugio — Tight End" in the 1982 TXSW Class 3A historical roster.
- Source: Billy Wilbanks, compiler (October 5, 2010), https://txswa.org/All-State%201921-2010/3-Football-1981-1995.pdf .

**Identity safeguard:** David Lewis is not automatically Calvin Lewis. No verified evidence connects the two names to the same individual. The compiled list supplies no personal receiving statistics or starts.

No authentic photograph is identified from this source; no conversation is certified cleared.

## Original 1983 Texas Relays — individually named relay participation

- **April 6–9, 1983, Austin, high-school boys 1600-meter relay (4×400 m), event 51, Heat 1:** University of Texas's original results name **David Lewis** as a Refugio runner alongside **Sterling Scott, Kent Harris and Jeffrey Dickerson**. Refugio placed **fourth in its preliminary heat, 3:30.84**. This is **not** a fourth-place overall final finish or an individual 400-meter mark.
- **Primary source:** [Original University of Texas 1983 Texas Relays results, event 51, Heat 1](https://texaslonghorns.com/documents/download/2019/6/19/TEXASRELAYS83Cont2.pdf). This track evidence does not change his separate 1982 All-State football honor or establish a relationship to Calvin Lewis. No authentic photo recovered.
