# Willie Mack Garza — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity.** Willie Mack Garza, Refugio High School football and track athlete; University of Texas defensive back; later football coach. Existing A1 Refugio Bobcats page explicitly names him as a person whose historical portrait belongs in the team gallery.

## Source-supported achievements

- **Refugio High School football:** three-time All-State selection and high-school All-American, running back. The Texas High School Football Hall of Fame reports **2,954 rushing yards in 16 games**, **56 touchdowns**, **336 points** in his senior season. [Texas Football Hall of Fame](https://www.texasfootball.com/hof-willie-mack-garza).
- **1987 football season / January 1988 All-State publication:** the January 10, 1988 *Polk County Enterprise* contemporary newspaper lists Willie Mack Garza, Refugio, senior, as a **Class 2A first-team running back and first-team defensive back**. This supports two positional selections in the same published list, not two different seasons. [Original newspaper scan/transcription, page 10](https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth795841/m1/10/).
- **1988 Texas High School Coaches Association All-Star Game:** selected, according to the Hall of Fame biography; selection is not by itself proof of playing time. [Texas Football Hall of Fame](https://www.texasfootball.com/hof-willie-mack-garza).
- **University of Texas:** football defensive back/cornerback, 1988 and 1990–1992; 1988 Southwest Conference Defensive Newcomer of the Year. [Texas Football Hall of Fame](https://www.texasfootball.com/hof-willie-mack-garza); [Montana State athletics coaching biography](https://msubobcats.com/sports/football/roster/coaches/willie-mack-garza/1051).
- **Texas High School Football Hall of Fame:** inducted in **2001**. [Texas Football Hall of Fame](https://www.texasfootball.com/hof-willie-mack-garza).

## Source discrepancy — preserve without inventing a resolution

The Hall of Fame biography calls the 2,954-yard, 56-touchdown season his senior season and also uses the phrase "in 1988"; the January 10, 1988 contemporary newspaper already identifies him as a senior, and other coaching biographies date that senior season to **1987**. This is a year-label discrepancy in sources, not proof of separate record seasons. [Hall of Fame](https://www.texasfootball.com/hof-willie-mack-garza); [January 1988 newspaper](https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth795841/m1/10/); [2019 coaching biography](https://svinews.com/2019/featured/33582/willie-mack-garza-joins-wyoming-cowboy-football-staff/).
