# Dana Robinson — A1 permanent individual record

**Documented association:** Refugio track-and-field athlete named in Toya Cardin Jones's existing A1 historical record.

- **1989 AAU Junior Olympics, boys ages 13–14, 100 m hurdles:** the existing Toya A1 research cites a **13.57-second first-place result** for Dana Robinson, with Toya Jones second in **13.99**. The existing research says the result was marked an AAU national record; the underlying original result sheet still requires independent review before the record designation is considered fully verified.
- **1993 UIL state 4×100 m relay:** existing Toya A1 page identifies the Refugio winning team as **Pete Perez, Roy Herron, Dana Robinson and Toya Jones**, with **41.3 seconds** in the state-meet account and **41.44** in a UIL record listing. Do not treat the two reporting formats as separate races.

**Source:** [Toya Cardin Jones's existing A1 record](../toya-cardin-jones/index.html), youth-track and high-school-track sections. This is an archive cross-reference to previously researched results, not a fresh independent reading of the original meet sheets.
