# Ray Anderson — A1 permanent individual record (Woodsboro, Texas)

## Source-documented football honor
- **1992 Associated Press Sports Editors Class 2A All-State, SECOND-TEAM defensive back**, **Woodsboro High School**, Woodsboro, Texas.
- Historical All-State compilation lists **“Anderson, Ray”**, **senior**, **170 lb**. This is a contemporary-season award preserved through a later roster compilation; the original 1992 AP publication remains to be recovered.
- Keep separate from unrelated athletes and administrators named Ray Anderson, including the former Stanford/Arizona State administrator and Australian football player.

**Source:** Billy Wilbanks, Texas Sports Writers Association historical All-State football compilation (2010), 1981–1995 volume, 1992 APSE Class 2A second-team defense: https://txswa.org/All-State%201921-2010/3-Football-1981-1995.pdf .

## Original December 15, 1992 AP newspaper — individual defensive production

The original *Big Spring Herald* APSE Class 2A All-State report explicitly identifies **“DB Ray Anderson, Woodsboro”**, **senior, 170 lb**, and prints **40 tackles and four interceptions**, along with **one forced fumble and five fumble recoveries** (the latter fumble figures are read from imperfect OCR and require page-image verification). These are published **season** statistics, not individual weekly game plays.
