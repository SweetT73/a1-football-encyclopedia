# Bryan Shaw — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Bryan Shaw, member of the **1968 Refugio High School** football team in Texas. The December 5, 1968 newspaper OCR sometimes renders his name **“Byran Shaw”** in a statistical table. Preserve that printed/OCR variant as an identification caution rather than creating a second person.

## Individually documented November 29, 1968 plays

The *Refugio Timely Remarks*, **December 5, 1968, p. 8**, reports Refugio's **21–19** bi-district win over Belton:
- During Refugio's third-quarter scoring drive, Eugene “Bull” Lewis threw two passes to Bryan Shaw, **one incomplete and the other completed for 14 yards**.
- In the fourth quarter, the report specifically credits **Bryan Shaw with recovering a Belton quarterback fumble** after James Connally had advanced to the Refugio six-yard line. The recovery started Refugio's eventual winning drive.
- The 1968 season rushing table lists “Byran Shaw” with **3 carries for 5 yards and 0 rushing touchdowns**; this is a **season-to-date** table, not a single-game total.

**Original source:** *Refugio Timely Remarks*, vol. 51, no. 12, Thursday, December 5, 1968, p. 8, “Bobcat Rally Wins Belton Game 21-19” and accompanying cumulative tables: https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth651690/m1/8/ .
