# Vance Claybrook — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity:** Vance Claybrook, member of the **1968 Refugio High School (Texas) football team**, identified by name in a contemporary newspaper game report.

## Individually documented defensive play

In the **November 29, 1968** Refugio–Belton bi-district game (Refugio won **21–19**), the *Refugio Timely Remarks* reports that **Vance Claybrook recovered a Belton fumble at the Refugio 11-yard line** after the Bobcats jarred the ball loose from Belton runner Robert Ford. The report **does not name Claybrook as the player who forced the fumble**. Do not assign him a forced fumble, tackle total, interception, or additional games.

**Original contemporary source:** *Refugio Timely Remarks*, vol. 51, no. 12, Thursday, **December 5, 1968**, p. 8, “Bobcat Rally Wins Belton Game 21-19”: https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth651690/m1/8/ .
