# Shane Lechler — A1 permanent individual record

**Identity and scope:** Shane Lechler, Texas A&M football punter. This entry documents an individual historical football action in a game involving Toya Cardin Jones; it is not a complete career biography.

## Source-verified play

- **September 12, 1998 — Texas A&M vs Louisiana Tech:** Texas A&M's original postgame notes identify junior All-America punter **Shane Lechler** as the punter on a **69-yard punt**, downed at Louisiana Tech's **1-yard line by Jason Webster and Toya Jones**. The same report states Lechler averaged **50.3 yards across four punts** that day. [Original Texas A&M Athletics postgame notes](https://12thman.com/news/1998/09/12/louisiana-tech-post-game-notes-quotes).
- **Record limits:** The 69-yard punt is Lechler's play; the downing is credited to Webster and Jones. Do not credit Lechler with either player's downing, or infer any unrelated season or career totals.

## 1995 McDonald's South All-Star football roster

- The original *Polk County Enterprise*, **July 30, 1995, p. 11A**, lists **Shane Lechler**, **No. 6**, **Class 2A**, **K (kicker)**, **6 ft 2 in**, **220 lb**, **East Bernard High School**, college destination **Texas A&M**, on the **South / White Jerseys** McDonald's Texas high-school all-star football roster. [Original newspaper](https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth790648/m1/11/).
- **Source limits:** Roster inclusion is verified, but game participation, kicking results, and actual jersey use on the field are not established. This is a **1995 high-school all-star listing**, distinct from the 1998 Texas A&M 69-yard punt already recorded above. The original newspaper's OCR sometimes renders his surname as “Lechier”; the standard spelling **Lechler** is supported by the 1998 Texas A&M original game notes.
