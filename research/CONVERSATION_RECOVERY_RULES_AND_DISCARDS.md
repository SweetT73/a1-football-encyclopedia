# MADDEN 27-A1 — DIRECT-TO-PERMANENT-FILE RULE
Effective: 2026-10-14

## Controlling filing order
For every item recovered from an A1 conversation, identify its proper permanent A1 destination and **save it directly into the file or subject folder where it belongs**. Verify the persistent save. Only afterward may a checkpoint, receipt, or handoff be created as a secondary convenience.

A checkpoint is NEVER the primary repository for recovered player information, historical facts, photographs, decisions, or corrections. Do not claim that writing a checkpoint fulfills permanent filing.

## Placement
- Player photographs: the established player's permanent photograph folder, with college/pro or other established classification.
- Team and group photographs: the established team/group destination and authorized cross-filings.
- Player history, numbers, dates, teams, ratings, appearance and equipment information: the appropriate permanent player record and established subject-specific records.
- General project rules and decisions: permanent A1 master rules/index.
- Historical research and other subject-specific information: its established permanent research or project file.
- Original images and source materials: preserved nondestructively.

## Required workflow
READ CONVERSATION → RECOVER CONTEXT AND APPROVALS → DETERMINE CORRECT PERMANENT DESTINATION → SAVE DIRECTLY THERE → VERIFY SAVE → REPORT → OPTIONAL CHECKPOINT → NEXT ITEM.
