Gale Sayers — The Kansas Comet

Chicago Bears · Running back · Historical video sources

This A1 player page connects two historical moments in Gale Sayers' career. The videos below were supplied as research references. Their full narration and footage have not yet been independently reviewed.

December 12, 1965 — Six touchdowns against San Francisco

Sayers scored six touchdowns in a 61–20 Bears victory over the San Francisco 49ers at Wrigley Field: four rushing, one receiving and one on a punt return. The field was muddy.

November 10, 1968 — Right knee injury against San Francisco

Chicago Bears vs. San Francisco 49ers, Week 9, Wrigley Field. Sayers sustained a serious right knee injury that ended his 1968 season. This footage is a potential companion to the previously supplied injury-aftermath photograph.

Watch the original 1968 game video on YouTube

Source video supplied by Sweet T. Specific footage of the injury has not yet been confirmed by direct viewing.

High school track and field

Sayers attended Omaha Central High School in Nebraska. His 1961 state high school long-jump mark of 24 feet 10½ inches stood for 44 years, until 2005. This Nebraska record is distinct from his later University of Kansas football career.
