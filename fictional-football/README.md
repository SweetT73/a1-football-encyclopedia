# A1 — Fictional American Football

A permanent A1 branch **parallel to real football history**, not a replacement for or subdivision of the actual NFL/college/professional histories.

## Canonical structure
- `teams/`: one permanent record for each fictional football franchise, with canonical roster and personnel links.
- `players/`: one canonical record per fictional athlete, linked to all teams, works and performances.
- `works/`: indexes of fictional football stories by original medium: theatrical films, television films, series/miniseries, books, plays, comics, and other media. Original release venue is separate from later streaming availability.
- `competitions/`: fictional leagues, schedules, seasons, opponents and standings; fictional results clearly distinguished from real competition.
- `evidence/`: scene stills, transcript findings, unresolved jersey numbers and cast identification.
- `visual-canon/`: verified performer/character likeness, period costume, jersey, helmet/equipment and appearance notes; never change identity for illustration.

The film, theatrical, literary, and television catalog keeps its own permanent index; cross-link the SAME team and player records rather than duplicating them. Real performers who played actual pro football link to their real A1 athlete files.

## Evidence discipline
Verified source, film dialogue/character claim, strong inference, and unknown are distinct. Film's real NFL setting does not make its fictional team's statistics historical NFL facts. Do not invent expansion status, displaced team, name, number, actor, measurements, stats, or franchise record. All fiction-context game results and career statistics must be marked in-universe.
