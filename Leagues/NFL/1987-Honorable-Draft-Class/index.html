Men who answered the call

The 1987 NFL players' strike began September 22 after two games of the regular season. One week's games were canceled. NFL clubs assembled other players to contest Weeks 4–6, and those games counted in the regular-season standings. Men came from colleges, other professional leagues, earlier NFL camps and working lives. Some were newly called in, while established veterans separately chose to cross the picket line.

The term replacement players is retained in historical citations because that was the official and commonly used designation. This encyclopedia calls the men Honor Bearers as Sweet T's tribute to their personal choice and achievement. The title expresses an editorial perspective, not an assertion that the labor dispute lacked legitimate grievances.

The strike involved genuine disagreement about employment rights and free agency; owners used nonunion rosters partly to weaken the strike. Those realities belong beside, not in place of, the biographies of people who played. Athletic opportunity, labor rights and personal choice must each be described accurately.

The language they faced

Some reporters, fans and striking players called the participants “scabs,” “scrubs,” or “strikebreakers.” Satirical nicknames mocked entire clubs: the “Spare Bears,” “Phoney-Niners,” “Shams,” “Dol-Finks,” “Sea-Scabs,” and “Saint Elsewheres,” among others. Those names belong in the historical record as evidence of the hostility the men encountered, not as this project's descriptions of their worth.

Documenting the ridicule does not diminish the rights of players who chose to strike. It asks readers to examine the people behind the labels and what each player actually accomplished.

Contemporary examples: Los Angeles Times, October 4, 1987; historical legal analysis of the strike.

Help recover lives beyond the box score

Former players, relatives, teammates, coaches, historians, reporters and fans can submit corrections or documented first-hand memories. This is not an unrestricted wiki: submissions are reviewed, sources are credited, and the permanent records are changed only after evidence is evaluated. Missing details stay unknown rather than being invented.
