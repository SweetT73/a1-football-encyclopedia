# Add a real 1987 NFL Honor Bearer history

This permanent section is intended to make overlooked football lives discoverable. Former participants, families, teammates, historians, coaches, reporters and fans are invited to help improve it.

**Open a suggestion:** https://github.com/SweetT73/a1-football-encyclopedia/issues/new

Include:
1. **Player's full name and 1987 team** (use its historical 1987 name); uniform number and position if known.
2. **What evidence you have:** dated team programs, newspaper scans, photos with photographer/provenance, NFL player pages, signed documents, first-hand recollections, school programs, college history, or interviews.
3. **The specific addition or correction** and which current claim it addresses.
4. **How you wish to be credited** and whether you have rights to share any submitted original images or writing.

A date-specific roster is evidence of roster membership; it does not, by itself, establish games played, starts or statistics. Evidence from teammates or family is welcome as attributed testimony and may require independent verification for statistical claims. A person may have had a career before or after 1987, including other football leagues and work outside sports.

Avoid posting private phone numbers, street addresses, medical records, identifying information about living relatives, or other nonpublic details. Do not upload copyrighted images or lengthy published text without authorization. You retain your own rights to original contributions; sending evidence for review does not transfer your rights or guarantee publication.

This is **not** an open-for-anyone-to-edit wiki. A1 editors review documentation, preserve credited sources and disagreements, correct typos, and update each athlete's one permanent profile without creating duplicate or temporary records. Disparaging historical nicknames can be documented with sources, but personal insults against past or present players are not accepted as contributions.
