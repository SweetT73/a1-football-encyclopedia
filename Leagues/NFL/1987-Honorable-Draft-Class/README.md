# The Honor Bearers — The Honorable Draft Class of 1987

**A1 permanent NFL section; created November 9, 2026.**

> They were never replacements, but rather men who seized the opportunity that history provided. They were the Honor Bearers, those who chose to answer the call.

## Permanent identity and editorial mission
"The Honor Bearers" is Sweet T's chosen tribute to real professional football players who took opportunities to join NFL strike-era rosters in 1987. "The Honorable Draft Class" is an encyclopedia research and historical fantasy-draft category, **not a claim that any player was selected in an actual 1987 NFL draft or that the class has been imported to a Madden game**. The historically used label "replacement" remains searchable in citation context only.

This one class covers **all 28 teams that played in 1987**, using their contemporaneous city and franchise names. The public site index is [index.html](index.html); the primary public factual registers are the 28 linked team chapters under [teams/](teams/). Personal biography records remain one person per canonical `athletes/<slug>/MASTER-RECORD.md`; team chapters cross-reference them, and never substitute for them.

## Source and status rules
- Roster listing is real roster evidence. **Do not require a statistical appearance, a recorded snap, or a start** for the team-membership achievement. Conversely, a pregame list alone does not prove that a person later dressed or played.
- Track `identified on dated roster`, `signed`, `dressed`, `appeared`, `started`, `statistics`, `returned established player`, and `unverified` separately.
- Do not count a veteran who crossed a picket line as a newly signed nonunion newcomer. They may have played in the same games, but the historical distinction matters.
- For NFL statistics, NFL.com and official team documentation take precedence over secondary totals. Secondary sites may aid discovery and independently sourced life history.
- The 1987 strike included legitimate labor issues, and owners used the strike-era roster strategy to apply pressure on organized labor. Our salute to individual participants never requires denying that context or asserting uniform motives.
- Document the period insults and team mock-nicknames by source and date. Never use them as this project's current names for these people.
- Preserve unknown and contested names and details without guessing; no fabricated photographs or invented family biographies. Original source images are not altered or deleted to produce derivative cutouts.
- **No temporary folders, duplicate individual records, or checkpoints**. This permanent section and each person's existing A1 biography are the destinations for additions.
- No alteration to the protected Chicago Bears 70-player fantasy roster, Madden ratings or EA College Football recruiting lists without a separate specific request.

## Research coverage

- 1,000 roster-name entries are transcribed into 28 chapters; the count is a sum of team-list names, not a claim that anyone belonged to multiple 1987 teams. Retain the supplied source attributions.
