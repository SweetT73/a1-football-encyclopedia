What happened here

Official Bengals history records a 1–2 mark in the strike-era games and identifies linebackers Reggie Williams and defensive end Eddie Edwards as established Bengals who crossed picket lines.

The 1987 strike canceled one week and was followed by three weekends of regular-season games played with strike-era squads and, in some cases, established players who crossed the picket line. Making a documented team roster is an achievement even when game statistics are absent; we do not infer dressing, snaps or starts from roster listing alone.
