What happened here

In 1987, New York Jets was one of the NFL's 28 teams involved in the owners' use of nonunion rosters during the players' strike. This chapter preserves dated evidence of its members and the way their participation was reported. Team-specific game accounts, first-person recollections and individual backgrounds are added only when supported by sources.

The 1987 strike canceled one week and was followed by three weekends of regular-season games played with strike-era squads and, in some cases, established players who crossed the picket line. Making a documented team roster is an achievement even when game statistics are absent; we do not infer dressing, snaps or starts from roster listing alone.
