Top 100 career leaders · Regular season · All positions · Historical record leaderboard

Ranking basis: total regular-season NFL rushing yards, including rushing quarterbacks where applicable. This is a statistical ranking, not a subjective list of the greatest running backs.

Research snapshot: October 18, 2026. Active-player figures are provisional and may change during the season. The original YouTube video supplied for this research is preserved below as a source lead, but the figures below are independently drawn from the Pro Football Reference leaderboard rather than attributed to an unverified video transcript.

NFL.com is the governing-body authority for career seasons and playing years. The season-count and playing-year review is complete for all 100 entries through the 2025 season, retaining previously confirmed work. Gaps in playing years are shown separately. Rankings and rushing-yard totals are preserved from the existing list; this review does not certify those totals or their cutoff.
