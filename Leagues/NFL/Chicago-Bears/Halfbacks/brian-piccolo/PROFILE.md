# Louis Brian Piccolo — Chicago Bears

**Permanent location:** A1 → Leagues → NFL → Chicago Bears → Halfbacks → Brian Piccolo.

**Primary historic position:** Halfback / tailback / running back. **Additional authentic playing position:** Fullback, 1969. **Bears jersey:** No. 41 (retired). **College:** Wake Forest. **Born:** October 31, 1943. **Died:** June 16, 1970.

## Playing career and position history

- **1965:** Chicago Bears taxi squad, no active regular-season games.
- **1966:** Mostly special teams, 3 carries for 12 yards.
- **1967:** Halfback backup to Gale Sayers; 317 rushing yards and 103 receiving yards.
- **1968:** Expanded playing role following Sayers's November knee injury; 450 rushing yards, his highest single-season total.
- **1969:** Practiced at fullback, and following a Ronnie Bull injury started at fullback alongside Sayers. This specific use **does not erase his usual halfback identity**.

Chicago Bears primary source: https://www.chicagobears.com/news/remembering-piccolo-50-years-after-his-passing
