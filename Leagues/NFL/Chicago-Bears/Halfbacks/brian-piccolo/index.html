Brian Piccolo

#41 · 1943–1970 · Wake Forest · Chicago Bears

Bears team · Gale Sayers · A1 home

Halfback and 1969 fullback

Piccolo backed up Gale Sayers at halfback in 1967, played a larger role when Sayers was injured in 1968, and started at fullback beside Sayers after Ronnie Bull was injured in 1969. His two roles belong to the same real athlete's record.
