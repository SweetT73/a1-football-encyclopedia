# John William Heisman — player, coach and football innovator

**A1 permanent location:** Leagues → College Football → Historical Coaches and Pioneers → John Heisman.

**Born:** October 23, 1869. **Died:** October 3, 1936.

## Playing years and affiliations

- **Brown University (1887–1889):** Early college and class football experience; lineman.
- **University of Pennsylvania (1890–1891):** Varsity football player, primarily on the line: guard, center, tackle and occasionally end. Studied law at Penn and received a degree in 1892.

University of Pennsylvania Archives: https://archives.upenn.edu/exhibits/penn-people/biography/john-william-heisman/

## Coaching and influence

Beginning at Oberlin in 1892, Heisman became one of American football's formative coaches. He coached at Oberlin, Buchtel/Akron, Auburn, Clemson, Georgia Tech, Penn, Washington & Jefferson and Rice, among others. He helped advocate for legalizing the forward pass, is associated with the early center-snap and vocal count, and contributed substantially to the development of football strategy. He was inducted into the College Football Hall of Fame in **1954**. The Heisman Trophy was named for him following his death.
