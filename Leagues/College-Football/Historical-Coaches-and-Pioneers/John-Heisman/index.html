John William Heisman

1869–1936 · Historical player and coach · College Football Hall of Fame (1954)

College Football · Brown · Penn

Playing career

Heisman attended Brown from 1887–1889 and played on Penn's varsity teams in 1890–1891, working on the offensive line at guard, center, tackle and sometimes end.

Coaching career

His coaching work from Oberlin through Georgia Tech, Penn, Rice and other schools helped change early American football, including its use of the forward pass and offensive formations. The Heisman Trophy was named for him after his death.
