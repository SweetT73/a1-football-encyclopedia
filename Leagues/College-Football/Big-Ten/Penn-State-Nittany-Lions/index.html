Joe Paterno: 46 seasons as head coach

After playing quarterback and cornerback at Brown University, Joe Paterno joined Penn State's staff in 1950. He was an assistant from 1950 through 1965 and head football coach from 1966 through 2011. Penn State won national championships in 1982 and 1986.
