# Penn State Nittany Lions — College Football

**Institution:** Pennsylvania State University (Penn State). **Not the same institution as the University of Pennsylvania (Penn Quakers).**

## Joe Paterno — coaching history

Joe Paterno played college football at **Brown University** and joined the Nittany Lions coaching staff in **1950**. He was an assistant coach **1950–1965**, then Penn State head football coach **1966–2011** (**46 seasons**). Penn State won national championships following the **1982 and 1986** seasons under his leadership.
