The Story of Aggie Football

Texas A&M football stretches from the program’s earliest years through the Southwest Conference era, the formation of the Big 12, and its move to the Southeastern Conference in 2012. A1 follows the team through the athletes and decisive plays that make those eras memorable, while connecting each documented player to a permanent biographical record.

Featured Championship Story

DECEMBER 5, 1998 · BIG 12 CHAMPIONSHIP · ST. LOUIS

The One-Yard Stop — And the Hit That Drove Him Back

With Kansas State threatening to steal the championship on the last play of regulation, quarterback Michael Bishop sent a pass toward the end zone. Everett Burnett caught the deflected ball near the goal line. Toya Cardin Jones met him there and drove him backward, keeping the Wildcats out of the end zone.

The play is remembered as the one-yard stop. But watch the collision: Jones drives Burnett backward toward the two-yard line, a detail called out in the television commentary. A&M went on to win 36–33 in double overtime, with Sirr Parker scoring the decisive touchdown.

ABC broadcast crew: Brent Musburger (play-by-play), Dan Fouts (analyst), and Jack Arute (sideline). Attribution of the exact two-yard-line remark remains unconfirmed.
