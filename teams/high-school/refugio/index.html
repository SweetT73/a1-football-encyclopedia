State Championship History

1970 · Co-Champions

Refugio 7, Iowa Park 7. The teams shared the Class 2A state title after a championship-game tie.

1982 · Champions

Refugio 22, Littlefield 21. A one-point victory secured the Class 3A championship.

2011 · Champions

Refugio 36, Cisco 35. Another one-point state championship victory, this time in Class 2A Division II.

2016 · Champions

Refugio 23, Crawford 20. The Bobcats claimed the Class 2A Division I title.

2019 · Champions

Refugio 28, Post 7. An undefeated season ended with the Class 2A Division I crown.

Football championship results: UIL historical state champions. The 1970 championship was shared, not an outright victory.

The Athletes Behind the History

James Lott — The High-Flying Bobcat

Before his University of Texas and international high-jump achievements, James Lott was a Refugio multi-sport standout. His high school football and track careers deserve to be told season by season, including the performances that made him one of the most remarkable athletes to come out of South Texas.

Willie Mack Garza — A Refugio Football Story

Willie Mack Garza belongs in the Bobcats’ athletic history, with his Refugio years connected to the college and professional stages of his career. A1 will distinguish documented playing achievements from coaching and later career accomplishments.

Toya Cardin Jones — From Refugio to a Big 12 Championship

A decorated Bobcat defensive back and track standout, Toya Jones went on to compete for Texas A&M in football and track and later played professional football. His tackle at the end of regulation in the 1998 Big 12 Championship helped keep Kansas State out of the end zone before the Aggies won in double overtime.

More Than Football

Refugio’s athletic history also includes individual and relay state championships in track and field. Those records belong alongside the football titles, with each event, mark, athlete, and year documented separately rather than combined into an unsupported school-wide total.
