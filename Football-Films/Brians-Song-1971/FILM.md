# Brian's Song (1971)

Original ABC television film, premiered **November 30, 1971**.

**James Caan:** Brian Piccolo. **Billy Dee Williams:** Gale Sayers. **Jack Warden:** George Halas. Directed by Buzz Kulik; teleplay by William Blinn.

The user-supplied image `11609.jpg` depicts Billy Dee Williams and James Caan **portraying** the players. Its sole retained original belongs in A1 Library → Football Films → Brian's Song (1971) → Photographs, with filename `Brians_Song_1971_Billy_Dee_Williams_James_Caan_11609.jpg`. It is not a historical photograph of the real Bears.
