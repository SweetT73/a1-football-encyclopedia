# Brian's Song (2001)

ABC television remake aired on *The Wonderful World of Disney* on **December 2, 2001**.

**Sean Maher:** Brian Piccolo. **Mekhi Phifer:** Gale Sayers. **Ben Gazzara:** George Halas. Directed by John Gray.

The film is separate from the 1971 original; no 1971 actors' photo should be filed as a 2001 remake still.
